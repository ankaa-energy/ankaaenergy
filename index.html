Our Mission

Pushing the

The next generation of unmanned aerial systems demands propulsion technology that doesn't yet exist at scale. Ankaa Energy was founded to close that gap — through rigorous scientific research, novel engineering, and an obsession with performance.

We focus on the most challenging frontier in aerospace: reliable, repeatable supersonic and hypersonic propulsion for autonomous platforms. Our work spans fundamental physics to operational hardware.

Research Focus

Where We Operate

F-01

Supersonic Propulsion

Development of turbojet, ramjet, and scramjet propulsion cycles optimized for UAV form factors operating above Mach 1.

F-02

Aerothermodynamics

High-fidelity CFD and wind tunnel testing of airframe-propulsion integration at transonic and supersonic speeds.

F-03

Propellant Chemistry

Research into advanced fuel formulations and oxidizer combinations for high-energy-density propulsion applications.

F-04

Thermal Management

Novel heat mitigation systems and materials science enabling sustained supersonic operation without structural degradation.

F-05

Autonomous Flight Systems

Guidance, navigation, and control architectures for unmanned platforms operating in contested and high-speed regimes.

F-06

Platform Integration

End-to-end vehicle design — from propulsion core to aerodynamic shell — for modular, mission-configurable UAV systems.

Propulsion Core

A variable-geometry inlet and combustion architecture that efficiently transitions from subsonic taxi to sustained supersonic cruise without sacrificing reliability or restartability.

Integrated Airframe

Airframe and propulsion co-designed from the ground up — eliminating the performance penalties of adapting existing airframes for high-speed propulsion packages.

Autonomous GNC

Onboard guidance, navigation, and control systems capable of handling the rapid aerodynamic changes inherent to transonic and supersonic flight envelopes.

Rapid Iteration Model

Simulation-to-hardware pipelines that compress the development cycle — enabling frequent test campaigns and continuous refinement of propulsion performance.
